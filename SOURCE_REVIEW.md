## How the material was used

Text extraction and visual review were combined because several older Khmer PDFs have imperfect embedded text encoding. The update synthesizes the teaching sequence, practical checklists, terminology and exercises. It does not copy screenshots or third-party illustrations from the slides.

The material was organized into five new course-native modules under `docs/field/`: map sheets and map use; GPS/GNSS; Avenza/offline mobile mapping; Google Earth/Maps; and Total Station-to-QGIS. New SVG diagrams and interactive teaching models were created locally and inherit the course license.

Interface instructions are phrased as durable workflows because application menus can change by version. Survey and GNSS sections distinguish teaching models from instrument accuracy and require field quality control.
